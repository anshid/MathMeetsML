# Mathematics and AI: your next steps

*MathMeetsML · "Your maths courses were never wasted."*

## 1. The idea in one line
AI does not remove the mathematician; it moves the value one level up: **frame** the problem, **model** it with explicit
assumptions, **verify** (proofs, tests, Lean), **interpret** the numbers, **explain** them to non-mathematicians.

## 7. Three habits that separate you from a button-presser
1. **Baseline first.** Before celebrating 97%, ask what a silly rule gets.
2. **State assumptions.** "A missed cancer costs 10 times an extra test" is a modelling choice; write it down.
3. **Check, don't trust.** A fluent answer, from a person or an AI, is not a correct answer. Test it, or prove it.
